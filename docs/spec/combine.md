# 6.4 Stitching (combine files)

Part of the Lectrix specification. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) holds the rules every change follows and [README.md](README.md) lists these files; sections keep the numbers they share, so "section 5.4" is in AGENTS.md and "section 6.5" in [annotations.md](annotations.md).

- A Combine view: add files (dialog or drag-and-drop), see them listed in a pane at the left and all their pages as thumbnails in one grid, and reorder, remove or rotate pages before combining.
- **Bookmark option** (default first): nest each source's outline under a new top-level bookmark named after the source's title or file name; or keep outlines flat; or drop them.
- **Page label option:** keep each source's labels (default); one continuous decimal sequence; or no labels.
- Annotations travel with their pages and still pass the interop profile afterwards.
- Internal links and named destinations are remapped to the new pages; colliding destination names and form field names are renamed with a per-source prefix, and the user is told how many were renamed.
- The result is always written to a new file via Save As. Source files are never modified.
- Also expose "Insert pages from file" into an open document, using the same engine.

## Settled details

- In the grid (`PageGrid.svelte`, shared with the page organizing tool), a click selects a page, Ctrl+click adds or removes one and Shift+click extends the selection. A page is dragged by its picture, and the selected pages go with it. A drag that starts anywhere else (a caption, the space between pages) draws a rectangle, which selects the pages whose pictures it touches as it grows: in place of the selection, or added to it with Ctrl or Shift held. Esc during a drag or a rectangle leaves everything as it was. A click on a caption selects its page. A selected page has a tint and a border around its whole block (its picture and what is under it), and the page with the keyboard focus a ring around that: in the accent in the page organizing tool, and in the colour of the page's file in the Combine view (`--page-cell-accent`). The columns are centered in the view, and so is a single row of fewer pages than fit.
- Under a page is its label alone (its place in its file after it where that says something else, "iv (4)"), not its file's name: the file is told by the colour a selected page has, and named in the page's accessible name. The page under the pointer tells it too: its block has a lighter tint of its file's colour and a thin edge in it, and a tooltip under the block names the file. Under the pointer, the label gives way to a bar in its place, centered under the picture: the same label, then buttons that turn that page counter-clockwise and clockwise and remove it. The bar has the measures of the bars that float over a page (an annotation's): their edge and shadow, and 32 px buttons with 16 px icons; the label's line is as tall as the bar (36 px), and 8 px of the block are under it, so the bar does not sit on the block's edge.
- The files and the options are in a pane at the left, which resizes from its inner edge as a side pane does (section 8): 180 to 480 px, 224 px at first and after a double-click on the edge, at most 40% of the window. Its width is remembered in app data with the panes.
- The list shows each file with its colour (six of them, in turn, as the files are added: `--lectrix-source-N`), its name and how many of its pages are in the result. A file is dragged up or down the list to another place: its name follows the pointer, a line shows where it would go, and Esc leaves it where it was. Alt+Shift+Up/Down do the same from the keyboard for the file with the focus, and Up and Down move between the files. Its pages go with it (`gatherPages` in `merge/pages.ts`): they come together, in the order they have, before the first page of the file listed after it (the next one with pages in the result; at the end without one), and the other pages stay as they are. One undo step, which puts the list back too. Dragging pages in the grid leaves the list as it is.
- The pictures change size as in the page organizing tool (section 6.7, `GridZoom`): the toolbar's Smaller pages and Larger pages buttons, Ctrl+= and Ctrl+-, Ctrl and the mouse wheel, and a touchpad pinch; the same sizes (160 px at first, up to one page filling the view), stepped and glided to the same way, in a grid that starts at its top left corner as that one does. The size is kept while the Combine view is open, and not remembered after.
- The Combine view is a tab of its own. Closing it asks first if pages were arranged but not combined. The result opens in a new tab, and the Combine tab stays open.
- Combining reads files as saved on disk. If one is open with unsaved changes, Lectrix offers "Save and combine", "Use saved version" or "Cancel".
- Files that forbid copying content are not added, with a plain message. Encrypted files that allow it ask for their password; the combined file is not encrypted.
- Before combining signed files, or inserting pages from one, Lectrix warns that the signature will not be valid in the result ("Combine anyway" / "Insert anyway", or Cancel). Signature fields are copied as they are.
- The same file can be added twice. Renamed names get a prefix by source position, the file's place in the list among the files with pages in the result ("src2_chap2"), or "inserted_" when inserting, plus a number if that is taken too.
- Top-level bookmarks follow the order of each file's first page in the result. "Each page keeps its label" numbers unlabeled files 1, 2, 3… from their first page; when the result would be 1, 2, 3… throughout, no `/PageLabels` is written.
- Insert pages: the pages go into the grid of the page organizing tool (section 6.7), which **Document > Insert pages from file…** shows first if it is not on screen. There they go after the page the document is at, or where the grid says (after the selected pages, at the place its menu names, into the gap a file is dropped on). The dialog asks which pages of the file and what happens to its bookmarks and page labels, and says where the pages go ("Into report.pdf, after page iv."); it does not ask where. Several files can be picked, or dropped, at once: the dialog then lists them with their page counts ("Insert pages from 3 files") and asks nothing about pages, since every page of each goes in, in the files' order, one after another. Each file is one undo step. A file's bookmark goes among the top-level bookmarks in page order.
- Not carried over: the structure tree, article threads, document-level JavaScript, open actions, viewer preferences and XMP metadata. The combined file is a plain full save.
