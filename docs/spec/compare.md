# 6.13 Compare

Part of the Lectrix specification. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) holds the rules every change follows and [README.md](README.md) lists these files; sections keep the numbers they share, so "section 5.4" is in AGENTS.md and "section 6.5" in [annotations.md](annotations.md).

Two versions of a document compared word by word, as ABBYY FineReader's Compare Documents compares them (ADR 0031): the differences listed and marked on both documents side by side, stepped through, ignored, and saved as a copy of either document with each difference highlighted and commented, or as a report. The documents compared are only read.

- **Where it is:** **Compare** on the start screen, after Compress, with two crossing arrows ("Find what changed"), and **File > Compare documents…**, after Combine files… (a command without a default shortcut, section 6.6). Either opens the Compare view, a tab of its own named "Compare documents" (one per window, as the Combine view is), or shows it if it is open. Opened with a document shown, that document is the first document (its file as saved: unsaved changes are saved first, if the user agrees, "Save your changes first?", **Save** and Cancel).
- **The view**, laid out as FineReader's: a bar at the top; the two documents side by side, the **first document** (the earlier version, as a rule) on the left and the **second** on the right, each in a pane with a header; and at the right the Compare panel, which resizes from its edge and keeps its width (`compareWidth`, 260 to 560 px, 320 at first, section 8.1).
  - **An empty pane** says which document it is for ("First document", "The earlier version, as a rule. Drop a PDF here, or open one."), with **Open…** (the Open dialog, "Open the first document"), and, when documents are open in tabs, "Or a document that is open:" and up to six of them by name (each one's file as saved, as above).
  - **Files dropped** on the view: one goes to the pane it is dropped on (or the first empty one); two fill both panes, the first on the left. While files are dragged over the window, the notice says "Drop one PDF on a side, or two to compare them". A file with a password asks for it, as opening does.
  - **A pane with a document** shows its pages as the viewer does (scroll modes as Settings set them, zoom, text selection and copying, links), read only: the Select tool whatever tool is picked, and annotations shown but not selected. Its header has "First document" or "Second document", the file's name (its path in the tooltip) and three buttons: **Save with the differences marked…**, **Open another document…** and **Remove**. The header of the pane last pressed carries the accent.
  - **The bar:** **Previous difference** and **Next difference** with "3 of 47", **Zoom out**, **Zoom in** and **Fit width** (on the pane last pressed; the other follows), **Scroll together** (a toggle, on at first), and **Swap the documents**.
- **The Compare panel** has two tabs, as FineReader's right-hand pane: **Compare** and **Differences** (with their count once compared).
  - **Compare:** **Compare pages by**, a dropdown: **Text and images (automatic)** (at first: text, and pages without text as images), **Text only**, **Images only**. Under **Find**, three checkboxes, all ticked at first: **Differences in punctuation** ("Commas, full stops, quotes and hyphens"), **One-letter differences** ("A word spelled differently by one letter"), **Headers and footers** ("Running titles and page numbers, listed apart from the text"); not available with Images only. **Swap the documents**. At the bottom **Compare** (the main button; **Compare again** once compared), available with both documents open, "Open both documents to compare them." above it otherwise. The options are remembered (Rust's store, `compare_settings`).
  - **While comparing:** a progress bar with "Reading pages… 120 of 500", "Comparing the text…", "Comparing pages as images… 3 of 18", and **Stop**, which ends it and keeps nothing ("Stopping…" until it has). A new comparison clears the last one.
  - **Differences:** a bar with "3 of 47" (or "47 differences"), **Previous difference**, **Next difference**, **Group the differences** (a toggle, on) and **Save the report…**. Under it the list, in three groups when grouped, each headed with its count: **Body text**, **Headers and footers**, **Numbering**; empty groups are left out. Each row has its kind's icon (plus, minus, pencil) in its colour, the text (deleted text struck through in red, inserted text in green, a change as "old → new"), three lines at most, and "Page 3 → 4" (page labels where the documents have them). With nothing found: "No differences found. The documents' text is the same, with the options chosen."
- **The marks** on the pages: inserted text in green, deleted in red, changed in yellow, a tint over the text in the document that has it, and a thin line of the same colour where the other document's text would go in the one that lacks it. The selected difference's marks have an edge. A text in a header or footer is marked as body text is.
- **Going to a difference:** a click on its row, or on its mark on a page, selects it; both panes glide to it (a click on a page moves only the other pane). **Ctrl+Right** and **Ctrl+Left** (commands **Next difference** and **Previous difference**, section 6.6), the bars' buttons, and Up and Down in the list go to the next and previous one listed. The first difference is selected when comparing ends. The row selected stays in view in the list.
- **Ignoring:** Delete in the list, or the row's context menu (**Ignore difference**, **Ignore identical differences**, which ignores every difference of the same kind with the same text in both documents), takes differences out of the list, the marks, the annotated copies and the report. Ctrl and Shift select several rows (Ctrl+A all of them). Under the list, "3 ignored" with **Show them** (listed dimmed, each with a button to put it back) and **Put all back**.
- **Copying:** Ctrl+C in the list, or **Copy difference** in the context menu, copies the rows selected as text: the kind, then each document's name, page and text.
- **Scrolling together:** while **Scroll together** is on, scrolling one pane scrolls the other to the same passage, by the places where the comparison lined the two documents up, so passages stay side by side across pages added or removed; between them the other pane moves in proportion. A pane keeps its distance from that place as it was when it began to lead, so moving one pane a little by hand stays. Before comparing, or without text, the pages of the same number. Going to a difference places both, and the next scroll starts from there. Zoom always goes together: the pane zoomed leads, a fit as a fit of the other's own pages.
- **Save with the differences marked…** (a pane's header) saves a copy of that document, from the Save dialog ("Save the document with the differences", "report (differences).pdf" in the document's folder), with each difference not ignored marked as FineReader marks them: the text this document has highlighted (one highlight along each line, in the kind's colour), its comment saying what the other has there ("Changed. In report-v2.pdf: “red”", "Deleted. Not in report-v2.pdf.", "Inserted. In report-v2.pdf: “, twice”"); where this document lacks the other's text, a sticky note at that place with the same comment. Written to the interop profile (section 5.1) by the document's author name (Settings), incrementally over the file's own bytes, so signatures stay valid. A notification says "Saved report (differences).pdf, with 46 differences marked. The document compared is unchanged." A file being compared can't be the target ("report.pdf is one of the documents being compared. Save under another name.").
- **Save the report…** saves a PDF report ("Comparison of report-v1 and report-v2.pdf", in the second document's folder): "Comparison report", the two documents' names, when they were compared, a summary ("47 differences: 12 insertions, 8 deletions, 27 changes."), the options compared with, and a table of the differences not ignored (number, type, page and text in each document), in the groups when the list is grouped. A4, laid out by MuPDF.
- **Closing** the tab (its close button, a middle click, Ctrl+W, **Close** in its context menu) closes the view and the documents it opened; nothing is asked, since nothing is changed. The title bar's icon and name show the start screen over it, as over the Combine view.

## Settled details

- **The view is FineReader's, not a joined tab:** a tab of its own, its panes outside the side panels, the commands and the annotation tools, which act on documents in tabs. Its documents are sources (section 6.4), opened again from their files even when they are open in a tab, and read only, as FineReader's are.
- **What is compared** is each page's text without its annotations (`Session::content_text`), in reading order, as words (Unicode word boundaries; CJK characters one by one). A word broken over two lines with a hyphen counts as one. Quotes, apostrophes, dashes and ellipses of any form compare as one; case counts. Spaces and line breaks do not: as in FineReader, missing or extra spaces are not differences, nor are fonts and formatting.
- **How the two are lined up:** the words of the whole documents, not page by page (so text moved to another page by an insertion is not a difference), by a patience diff anchored on words found once in both, with Myers' shortest edit script between anchors within a budget (`compare/diff.rs`). A stretch of up to three equal words between two changes, at most half as long as either, joins them into one change; insertions and deletions never join across equal text.
- **Headers and footers** are lines in the top or bottom 12% of a page whose text, numbers taken as any number, is near the top (or bottom) of another page too. They are compared apart from the body, so a page number shifted by an inserted page is a difference in their group, not in the text's. **Numbering** is a change of nothing but a list's numbers or bullets at the start of a line.
- **One-letter differences** are a word changed alone into a word one letter from it. Off, they are not listed. **Punctuation** off leaves punctuation out of the comparison altogether.
- **Pages as images:** both pages rendered in grey at 100 dpi, laid one over the other from their top-left corners, cells of 6 pixels differing where 4 of their pixels differ by 64 grey levels or more, and cells within 3 of each other taken together; at most 40 areas a page pair, more taken as one. In the automatic mode, a page with fewer than three words is compared with the page the text lines up with it (the page of the same number without text to go by); pages past the other document's last are inserted or deleted whole.
- **The annotated copy's marks** are Highlight annotations with a comment and Text (sticky note) annotations, which the interop profile allows (section 5.1, rule 1). FineReader's help says only that each difference is "highlighted and commented"; Acrobat's Caret annotation is not in the profile.
